## Acceptance checks
- Hover a short audio clip, press and drag **slowly** left/right for 1, 2, 10 cells, zoom timeline and repeat. Release outside track; Undo/Redo must restore original start.
- Press and hold the left mouse button on frame-number ruler, move across contiguous frame labels and release. Current frame must track pointer without painting on the stage or dragging the entire timeline.
- Scrub in OLD, NEW and Advanced Timeline; Advanced keyframe group drag still works.
- Verify no unrelated application names remain in visible fill controls and customer-facing release notes; internal serialized enum variant naming stays unchanged for compatibility.
- Enable Onion Skin, draw beyond the unrotated paper, confirm the preview clips; rotate the paper and note the current rectangular clip limitation. Exports and saved strokes must remain unchanged.
- Host a LAN room, copy/paste a Stick invite and verify connection with a second machine. Test ZeroTier/private network in a separate setup if available. Ensure no password is included in the invitation and unauthorized hosts cannot read files.
- Check latest release consists of Stick.exe, version.txt and SHA256SUMS.txt and that Windows CI passed.
- Settings → Previous Versions must show the immediate previous verified release plus up to 10 older verified releases, without duplicates.

## Network safety
The existing protocol is host-owned direct TCP with password challenge but does not provide end-to-end content encryption. Sharing a publicly reachable IPv4 endpoint exposes a TCP listener to the internet; strongly prefer a trusted private network until a separately audited encrypted relay service is introduced. Google Pages is static hosting and cannot be treated as a room relay.
