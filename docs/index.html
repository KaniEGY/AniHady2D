Optional Google Drive backup

With your consent, save artist progress and locally saved project files to the app's private Google Drive app-data area. Restore copies explicitly.

Google sign-in & data access

Google sign-in opens in your regular browser. Stick Studio requests openid, email, and drive.appdata for account identification and optional backups. It does not need access to your other Google Drive documents. Projects remain local if you do not enable cloud backup. Read our Privacy Policy for specific details on data, retention, deletion and access.
