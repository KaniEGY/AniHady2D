PRIVACY & GOOGLE USER DATA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026. This policy explains how the Stick Studio Windows desktop application handles information, including information accessed using Google APIs.

1. Who we are

Stick Studio is a 2D drawing and animation application for Windows. For privacy questions or data requests, contact capcut2026yt26@gmail.com. The official project is available at github.com/KaniEGY/Stick.

2. Google information and permissions

Google sign-in is optional and launches in your external browser through Google's OAuth authorization process. The app requests the following permissions:

  • openid: identify the Google account uniquely using an account identifier.
  • email: display the email address of the account you connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: create, read, update and retrieve only this app's private application-data files in your Google Drive.

Stick Studio does not request access to browse your general Google Drive files, emails, contacts or other unrelated Google account data. It never asks you to type your Google password into the app.

3. What we save to Google Drive and when

Only after you sign in and choose a backup action, or explicitly enable automatic cloud backups, Stick Studio can upload an artist profile containing your display name, earned XP and drawing statistics, optionally a reduced-size avatar image, and copies of existing locally saved Stick project files. The app stores these backups in the private appDataFolder of your own Google Drive account. They are not normally visible in your regular My Drive file list. Uploaded project files may contain artwork and information you chose to include in a project. Any media referenced by local file paths outside the project document is not currently bundled into backups.

Cloud backup is off by default. Saved projects and the artist profile remain on your computer if you do not sign in or enable/upload backups. Cloud restore is a deliberate user action; downloaded projects are stored as separate copies rather than replacing the active original.

4. Local storage and account protection

The program stores local preferences, recently opened project locations and artist progress on your Windows account. It saves the selected Google account identifier and email locally, while the Google OAuth refresh token is stored in Windows Credential Manager rather than in project files or the public website. A time-limited access token is requested from Google when required. Signing out disconnects the account on this computer and removes its stored authorization, but does not automatically delete backups already present in Google Drive.

5. Online collaboration and other connections

Optional online rooms connect participating computers directly or through a network such as LAN or a separately configured Tailscale connection. When you join or host, Stick may exchange your displayed artist name, self-reported level, participant information and the drawing/project edits needed for the collaborative room with other participants. Networking is only enabled for a room session when you choose it. Current room traffic is not end-to-end encrypted by Stick Studio. The updater contacts GitHub to check for official releases, download updates and verify their cryptographic hashes. The public website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub's hosting practices.

6. Purpose, sharing and limited use

Google user information is used only to connect your chosen account, perform the Google Drive backup/restore features you request, and show which account is connected. Stick Studio does not sell Google user data, use it for advertisements, or transfer it to data brokers. Data can be transmitted to Google's APIs to provide the features, and to other room participants only when collaboration is explicitly used. Google API data is handled according to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention, access and deletion

Locally saved projects and preferences remain on your computer until you remove them. Application backups remain in your Google Drive private app-data storage until you delete them, revoke the application's access and remove associated data, or delete the Google account. Signing out does not automatically delete existing Drive backups. You may revoke access from your Google Account connections page, and contact us at capcut2026yt26@gmail.com for help with app data deletion. A future version may provide additional in-app cloud deletion controls; these are not promised as available today.

8. Security, minors and changes

Stick Studio uses browser-based Google OAuth with PKCE, the Windows credential store and encrypted HTTPS connections to Google's services. No software can guarantee absolute security; keep your system updated, use account security features and back up important projects. Stick Studio is a general creative application and is not specifically directed to children. We may update this policy as functionality changes, with the date revised on this page.
